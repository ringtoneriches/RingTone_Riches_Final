halloween-moon.webp
===================

The Moon's near side, rendered from NASA's CGI Moon Kit.

Source:  NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, "CGI Moon Kit"
         https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
File:    lroc_color_2k.jpg (2048x1024 equirectangular colour map), built by
         the LRO camera team from over 100,000 Wide Angle Camera images.

Credit line NASA asks for:
         "NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio"

Licence: NASA media is generally not subject to copyright in the United
         States and may be used for commercial purposes, provided the use
         does not imply NASA endorsement of a product or service. We use it
         only as scenery for a seasonal theme, which does not.
         https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

What we changed:
         Projected orthographically from the equirectangular map onto a disc
         (the map is the whole sphere unrolled and cannot be laid on a circle
         without smearing the poles), given a gentle limb fall-off, warmed
         very slightly, feathered at the edge, and saved as WebP with alpha.
